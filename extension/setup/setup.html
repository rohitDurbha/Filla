Filla

Welcome to Filla

Filla tailors your resume with your own Claude for every job, then fills the application. Setup takes about five minutes, all on this page. First, start the engine.

In the Filla folder you downloaded, double-click start-mac.command on Mac or start-windows.bat on Windows, and keep that window open. Then check below.

Local server starts with the double-click
Tectonic LaTeX compiler, brew install tectonic
Claude Code installed and logged in with your Claude account

About you

Filla reads your public GitHub so Claude can pick your most relevant projects for each job.

Your resume tracks

A track is one base resume you apply with, like Data Science or Software Engineering. Paste the LaTeX source of each, or upload the .tex file.

Your rules for Claude

These rules drive every tailoring run. Say what Claude may rewrite, what stays frozen, how projects get picked, and any layout limits. The better the rules, the better the resumes.

One-time application answers

US applications ask these on nearly every form and they never appear on a resume. Answered once, filled everywhere. Stored only in your browser. Picking Skip leaves the question untouched.

Filla is ready

Open a job posting on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or SmartRecruiters and click the F chip on the right edge of the page. Keep the server window open while you apply. Your application history lives at localhost:8765/tracker.
